Claude Gmail

A personal tool. Not offered to the public.

Claude Gmail is a personal tool that connects its owner’s own Gmail account to Claude, an AI assistant, running on the owner’s own computer. It lets the owner search and read their email, organize it with labels, and prepare drafts and daily summaries.

It is used only by its owner. There is no sign-up, no service offered to other people, and nothing for sale.

This site exists so the tool’s Google sign-in screen can link to a description and policies, as Google requires.
